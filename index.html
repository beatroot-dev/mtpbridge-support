Help & contact

Your files.
A direct USB connection.

MTPBridge browses and transfers files between your Mac and a USB device that exposes MTP storage.

Report a bug or ask a question

In the app, choose Help → Report a Bug…, or write to us directly. Your email app opens a draft; you choose whether to send it.

Email support

beat.root.dev@icloud.com

What to include

  • The app version, macOS version and device model.
  • The device's MTP software and version, if applicable.
  • What you tried, what happened and what you expected.
  • A screenshot or diagnostic log, if it helps. Review it first: logs may contain device names, file names and paths.

Open the log with Help → Show Diagnostic Log. Attachments are optional. Please do not send passwords, keys, personal documents or unrelated files.

Connect a device

  1. Use a USB cable that supports data transfer.
  2. On Android, unlock the screen, select File Transfer / MTP and grant access if prompted.
  3. Click Connect and choose a folder on your Mac.

If the device is busy, close other file transfer and photo import apps, reconnect the USB cable and restart the device's MTP mode.

Compatibility

Requires macOS 26 or later and an Apple Silicon Mac. Compatibility depends on the device's MTP implementation.

Privacy and open source

USB file transfers stay between your Mac and connected device. Read our privacy policy for local logs, support email and this website.

Choose Help → Open Source Licenses… to find libmtp and libusb licenses, authors and rebuilding instructions. Their source archives are included in the application bundle.
